# Sensitive Memory Allows Stated Facts, Not Inferred Labels

Sensitive domains are not categorically excluded from long-term Memory. A
direct, unambiguous, self-referential User statement about health or diagnosis,
sexual orientation, religious or political identity, trauma, or a financial
or legal situation may create an `objective + confirmed` Claim when an active
Sensitive Memory Predicate explicitly permits it and the normal long-term
admission checks pass. `confirmed` records what the User stated; it does not
claim independent verification.

An AI Impression may interpret the meaning or personal impact of a sensitive
experience, its role as a source of support or stress, or the User's preferred
way of discussing it. It may not infer and persist a medical or psychiatric
diagnosis, sexual orientation, religious or political identity, trauma or
abuse label, or financial or legal risk classification that the User did not
directly state. Symptoms, partner relationships, religious activity, political
views, and repeated difficulties are not substitutes for that statement.

The Predicate Registry enforces this boundary per stable question and Claim
kind rather than banning entire topics. Sensitive classification Predicates
admit only the direct Objective path; sensitive interpretive Impressions use
separate Predicates with their own stricter Evidence and review policies. This
preserves understanding and empathy without turning correlated expressions
into persistent sensitive labels.
