# Bio Budget Overflow Is Disclosed, Not Fatal

Bio must remain useful when the full User Profile no longer fits a readable narrative. A versioned coverage planner applies Bio Coverage Class and stable policy ordering before generation, producing the coverage plan for that BioVersion. The writer may choose wording and compression only inside that plan. Units that do not fit are recorded as `deferred_by_budget`, not silently dropped or mislabeled as `material_absence`; the published Bio exposes a user-facing indication that it presents a selected part of SayHear's current understanding.

Validation still checks every planned `required` and `compressible` unit, all proposition support, and contradictions against the complete eligible Claim set. Budget pressure never permits an unsupported statement or a Bio that reverses the meaning of omitted active Memory. This preserves a readable, available product without returning materiality authority to the model or failing the whole Bio merely because User Profile has grown.
