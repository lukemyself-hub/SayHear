# Humans Govern Predicate Registry Expansion

An LLM may return an `ambiguous / unmapped` MemoryCandidate with Evidence,
mapping alternatives, and a proposed concept, but it cannot decide that the
concept has product value, approve a Predicate, or publish a Registry version.
A versioned deterministic policy decides only whether the Candidate is
Predicate Review Eligible by checking Evidence validity, stable cross-day
meaning, whether the existing Registry can already express it, long-term
Memory boundaries, and whether a reviewable schema can be defined.

Predicate Review Eligible means only that an authorized human product reviewer
may evaluate the proposal; it does not create an active Claim or establish
product value. The reviewer may map it to an existing Predicate, approve a
complete new Registry Entry, or reject long-term memorization. LLMs may assist
with clustering, ranking, confusable searches, and draft examples, while final
approval and Registry publication remain explicit human governance. A
Candidate still passes normal mapping and admission after a new Registry
version is published.
