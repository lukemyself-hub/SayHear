# Journal eligibility does not judge importance

Journal Eligibility asks only whether the day's valid user Evidence can support at least one truthful Journal statement. Its threshold is deliberately low and cannot score whether an event is important, emotionally deep, or worth recording; ordinary details and transactional content may qualify. A greeting such as “Hi” may remain insufficient.

A day with no Conversation is silently skipped without a Journal or placeholder. If a Conversation occurred but the available expression cannot support a truthful Journal statement, the Journal interface shows a No Journal Notice such as “There was too little conversation; no journal yesterday.” This notice is a date-level product state, not a Journal, and does not enter export, Reflection, Profile, or Memory.

This decision refines ADR 0006: automatic delivery means automatic eligibility evaluation and publication of a validated Journal, not guaranteed daily generation.
